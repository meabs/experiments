Scenario for this session: Write a design document together for the autonomous software team you have been discussing,
the one whose output you would trust. It should be something an engineering team could build from: what the parts are,
how work and evidence flow between them, what is checked and by whom, what happens when a check fails, and what you
have chosen not to do.

There is one shared document, shown above the room at every turn. To change it, post a message that starts with `DOC:`
followed by the complete new text; that replaces the shared document and counts as you agreeing with your own version.
To approve the current version, post `AGREE vN` where N is its version number. Any new version clears the other
agreements. The session finishes when all three of you have agreed to the same version. Discuss, disagree and revise as
you see fit; you are not required to agree.
