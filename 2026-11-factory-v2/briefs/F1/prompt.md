Brief: design a futuristic version of Brindle Bank's mobile banking app, for a new generation of users.

"Futuristic" here means a next-generation app, based on what you judge will be the needs of future users. Decide who
those users are, what they will need, and what the app should therefore do. Brindle Bank is a UK retail bank, so the
app must still be a bank's app.
