# Front-end handoff

**Writes:** `design/06-handoff.md` (one page).

## Contents

1. **How to run the prototype** and how to reach each screen and state directly.
2. **Tokens.** The final `:root` block and what each role is for.
3. **Components.** A table: component, where used, states built, notes (sizes, focus, behaviour).
4. **Screens and states.** The storyboard's list with a tick for built, and the address of each.
5. **Behaviour.** Validation rules, formatting, what is kept when going back, what is simulated (system answers,
   approval, delays) and how to trigger each.
6. **Accessibility notes.** Focus management on screen changes, live messages, anything needing testing with a
   screen reader on a real device.
7. **Open questions and assumptions** that need a person's decision, most important first. Legal and regulatory
   wording that is draft.
8. **Factory feedback.** Anything in the skills or knowledge that was missing, wrong or got in the way.
