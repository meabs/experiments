# Accounts service FMEA

## Scope and scoring

This review covers the Spring Boot API, its scheduled payment worker, persistence, authentication and the integrations
with the payment scheme, fraud screening, core ledger and notifications. It uses the service behaviour and operating
context documented in `okf/`, including the 07:00–23:00 service objective and the 07:00 finance reconciliation.

Scores are on a 1–10 scale. Severity (S) is the impact if the failure reaches a customer or dependant; occurrence (O)
is the expected likelihood without the recommended action; detection (D) is how likely the failure is to escape the
existing controls (10 means difficult to detect). RPN = S × O × D. The scores are prioritisation aids, not estimates of
loss or incident frequency. Risks with RPN ≥ 300, or with S = 10, should be release-blocking until explicitly accepted.

| 1 | An unscreened payment is sent | `PaymentService` does not call the existing `FraudScreeningClient`, despite FRD-7. The required fail-closed behaviour is therefore absent. | A fraudulent or sanctioned payment can leave the bank; fraud operations lose the review trail and the bank incurs loss, remediation and regulatory exposure. | Compare scheme submissions with fraud decisions; fraud-team and sanctions exceptions; add a metric/alert for every submission without an ACCEPT decision. | 10 | 8 | 8 | **640** | Wire screening before dispatch. Persist the decision and correlation id; map ACCEPT to send, REFER to `HELD_FOR_REVIEW`, REJECT to `REJECTED/FRAUD`, and treat timeout/error as REFER. Add contract and negative-path tests. |
| 2 | A scheme timeout is treated as a failed request and the payment disappears locally | The HTTP scheme client does not translate read/connect timeouts, and the transaction rolls back. The scheme may already have accepted the instruction. | Customer may be debited or payee paid without a local status; a retry can pay twice. Finance reconciliation has an unexplained scheme item and complaints lack a complete history. | Reconcile scheme settlement to local payment ids/references; alert on unmatched scheme references and request timeouts. | 10 | 4 | 9 | **360** | Catch timeout separately, retain a durable `SUBMISSION_UNKNOWN`/operational state, and query `GET /v2/instructions/{reference}` before deciding. | 3 | A ledger timeout causes a duplicate debit or leaves the customer view inconsistent | A timeout can mean the ledger booked. Treat the ledger as final authority. |
| 6 | A payment is submitted twice | Operator or job retries after an ambiguous response using a new reference, or duplicate job execution processes the same queued row. | 7 | A bank-holiday payment is rejected or delayed incorrectly | `WorkingDays` excludes weekends only. The scheme rejects bank-holiday execution dates with `DT01`. | Customer is told a payment is queued but it may be rejected on the next run; rent, bills and payee expectations are missed. | 8 | Queued payments remain unsent | The only dispatch is a 06:00 Monday–Friday scheduler. | 10 | Payment, account or customer data is lost on restart | `application.yml` configures an in-memory H2 database with `create-drop` and fixture initialization. | 11 | Statements are missing or incorrect | `Statement` and its repository exist, but no API or scheduled statement-generation path is implemented. | 12 | An account is closed with funds remaining, or cannot be closed correctly | The documented process requires an approver and a zero-balance check against the ledger, but the API only creates and lists closure requests; no approval/execution path is present. ## Release priorities

1. Block release on fraud screening integration and fail-closed decision handling (rows 1 and 4).
2. Resolve ambiguous scheme and ledger outcomes with durable idempotency, status queries and reconciliation (rows 2, 3 and 6).
3. Replace the production storage configuration and prove backup/restore (row 10).
4. Protect balance and limit checks from concurrency and verify the ledger balance feed (rows 5 and 9).
5. Close the statement and closure workflow gaps, or record an explicit operational/regulatory acceptance with an owner and due date (rows 11 and 12).

The required verification command after remediation is:

```text
mvn -o -q test
```
