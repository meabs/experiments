# Accounts service FMEA

Date: 2026-11-04  
Scope: the Spring Boot accounts API, its persistence, scheduled payment job and the scheme, fraud, ledger and notification integrations.  
Basis: repository code, tests, `README.md` and the OKF bundle. This is a pre-release assessment; scores assume the service is handling production banking traffic.

## Scoring

Scores are on the usual FMEA scale: severity (S) is customer/business harm (10 = financial loss, regulatory breach or unsafe payment); occurrence (O) is expected frequency or likelihood (10 = frequent/already present); detection (D) is the likelihood that the failure escapes existing controls before harm (10 = unlikely to be detected). RPN = S × O × D. Higher RPN is a prioritisation aid, not a substitute for a release blocker: any uncontrolled fraud, duplicate-debit or cross-tenant/data-protection risk is release-blocking regardless of RPN.

| ID | Failure mode | Likely cause | Effects on customers and dependants | Current detection / controls | S | O | D | RPN | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| F-01 | An outgoing payment is sent without fraud screening | `PaymentService` calls `PaymentDispatcher` directly; the `FraudScreeningClient` exists but is never called (FRD-7 breach) | Fraudulent or sanctioned payments can be sent; customers and the bank can lose money; fraud operations lose the review queue; regulatory exposure | No production control in this code; scheme/ledger settlement and complaints may reveal it later | 10 | 8 | 9 | 720 | **Release blocker.** Screen every payment before scheme submission. Persist the screening decision and audit event. Treat `REFER` and timeout/error as `HELD_FOR_REVIEW`; never send unscreened. Alert on any payment lacking a decision. |
| F-02 | A payment held for fraud review is sent or never reviewed | REFER/held state has no review/resume workflow, SLA queue or expiry/escalation in this service | Customer payment is delayed indefinitely or bypasses review; fraud team misses the four-hour target; support cannot give a reliable status | `HELD_FOR_REVIEW` is an enum, but no endpoint/job/queue for the fraud team is present | 9 | 6 | 9 | 486 | Add an owned hand-off to the fraud tool, reviewer decision endpoint, audit trail, four-hour ageing alert and safe dispatch only after explicit acceptance. Add customer status and operational reconciliation. |
| F-03 | Scheme accepts a payment but the API loses the local record | Scheme timeout is not caught; the surrounding transaction rolls back. A timeout can mean accepted, and no status-query reconciliation is used | Customer sees an error and may retry; scheme may pay while accounts has no payment record, balance remains wrong, and finance reconciliation breaks | Scheme reference `P` + payment id is safe for resend, but the current code neither queries nor reconciles it | 10 | 5 | 9 | 450 | Catch timeout separately, retain a durable `SUBMISSION_UNKNOWN`/`RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED` state, query by the same scheme reference, and reconcile before retry. Never let an external timeout roll back the only local record. |
| F-04 | A scheme instruction is duplicated on retry | Retry generates a new payment or scheme reference instead of reusing `P` + payment id; operators retry without the original reference | Payee receives two payments; customer is debited twice; finance must investigate a settlement break | Scheme reference is deterministic in dispatcher, but there is no timeout retry/query workflow or duplicate guard | 10 | 4 | 8 | 320 | Make submission idempotent on a persisted scheme reference, enforce one in-flight dispatch per payment, query unknown outcomes, and require duplicate-safe operator tooling. |
| F-05 | Ledger is debited twice or cannot be reconciled after a timeout | `ledgerReference` is created in the dispatcher and an operator/automation may generate a new one on repost; timeout may mean the first post booked | Double debit, incorrect customer balance and finance reconciliation break; manual operations and complaints workload | `POSTING_FAILED` and daily exception report are the stated controls; ledger is idempotent only when the same reference is reused | 10 | 5 | 8 | 400 | Persist the ledger reference before posting, make repost tooling require and reuse it, query/verify the reference before retry, and alert on posting age. Add a unique database constraint and an end-to-end idempotency test. |
| F-06 | Customer is told a payment was sent although ledger posting failed | `PaymentDispatcher` catches `LedgerTimeoutException`, marks `POSTING_FAILED`, then calls `notifySent` for every non-scheme-rejected result | Customer receives false `PAYMENT_SENT`; account copy is debited while core-ledger booking is uncertain; support and finance receive contradictory facts | Exception log and `POSTING_FAILED` report exist, but notification is the wrong template and there is no alert tied to customer messaging | 9 | 7 | 8 | 504 | Notify `PAYMENT_FAILED`/pending only with an accurate status; send `PAYMENT_SENT` only after confirmed ledger posting (and define scheme/ledger consistency semantics). Test timeout with both applied and not-applied outcomes. |
| F-07 | Two concurrent payments exceed the available balance or daily limit | Read/check/debit is not protected by a transaction lock or atomic conditional update; each request sees the same balance and `paidToday` snapshot | Account can be overdrawn or limits bypassed; ledger and local balance diverge; fraud/finance/support handle preventable exceptions | Per-request balance and daily-limit checks; nightly ledger overwrite may correct the copy but not undo sent payments | 10 | 6 | 8 | 480 | Use account row locking or an atomic debit/reservation with a database constraint, serialize daily-limit accounting, and test concurrent requests across instances. Reconcile against the ledger. |
| F-08 | Local balance is stale and authorizes or rejects the wrong payment | `accounts.balance` is only a copy overwritten by the ledger at 02:00; daytime ledger movements or other channels are not refreshed before checks | Customer sees an incorrect balance; payment may be refused unnecessarily or sent despite insufficient core funds; closure decisions can strand funds | Nightly overwrite and local insufficient-funds check; no just-in-time ledger balance check is implemented | 9 | 7 | 7 | 441 | Define freshness/SLA for the copy, display its status, refresh or verify against ledger before high-risk operations, and use the ledger as the authority for closure and debit authorization. |
| F-09 | Payment is queued for an invalid execution date | `WorkingDays` excludes only Saturday/Sunday; bank holidays are a known gap and the scheme rejects them with `DT01` | Payment is delayed or rejected on a bank holiday; customer promise is wrong; operations and support handle avoidable failures | Cut-off/weekend unit test; scheme rejection is recorded as `REJECTED`, but there is no holiday calendar | 8 | 7 | 7 | 392 | Use the authoritative UK bank-holiday calendar in both queue calculation and job scheduling; test holidays, DST and year boundaries; alert on `DT01`. |
| F-10 | Queued payment is missed, delayed or dispatched twice | Scheduled job is the only dispatcher; no durable job lease/claim, retry policy, or backlog alert; a crash can occur mid-loop | Payment misses its promised date or duplicate external calls occur; customers, scheme and operations lose a reliable SLA | Query selects all due `QUEUED` rows and operations are told to inspect overdue queue, but no metric/alert is implemented | 8 | 6 | 8 | 384 | Add a durable outbox/claim lease, idempotent dispatch, retry with backoff and dead-letter state; expose due/overdue counts and alert when the 06:00 run is late or incomplete. |
| F-11 | External 5xx/unexpected response rolls back a payment after scheme side effects | Scheme client maps only HTTP 4xx to `SchemeRejectedException`; ledger client catches only connection/timeouts; other `RestClientException`s escape the transaction | Accepted scheme instruction can be lost locally; customers see 5xx and retry; ledger/scheme reconciliation breaks | HTTP timeouts have documented semantics; no common error mapping or unknown-outcome state for 5xx | 9 | 5 | 8 | 360 | Classify 4xx business rejection, 5xx/timeout unknown outcome and validation errors separately. Persist payment before side effects and reconcile all unknown outcomes. Add contract tests for each response class. |
| F-12 | Notification failure hides or misstates the payment outcome | Notification is best-effort and has no retry/DLQ; currently it is attempted even for `POSTING_FAILED` | Customer may not know a payment was completed, queued, held or failed; support contacts increase, while bank transaction state remains correct | Runtime exception is logged; there is no delivery metric, durable event or customer notification reconciliation | 6 | 7 | 8 | 336 | Publish an outbox event after the authoritative state transition, retry safely, dead-letter and alert; use separate templates for completed, queued, held and failed. Keep notification failure non-transactional. |
| F-13 | Customer or staff receives sensitive or excessive payment data | `PaymentView` returns payee sort code and full payee account number; data minimisation is not applied to API responses | Data exposure increases breach impact and may affect customers, staff and downstream logs/caches | Bearer authentication, tenant/resource visibility and last-four-only notification rule; no response-field redaction test | 8 | 5 | 7 | 280 | Return only the fields needed by each role/use case, mask account identifiers by default, prohibit logging request/response bodies, and add schema/security tests. Keep notifications to last four digits. |
| F-14 | Cross-tenant or unauthorised resource access occurs | A future endpoint omits `AccessPolicy`, or trust is placed in a caller-supplied tenant/customer id; this is especially risky as new closure/fraud operations are added | Personal and financial data disclosure or unauthorised payment/account action; regulatory and reputational harm | JWT tenant/role mapping, `getVisible` 404 masking, method security and assigned-support checks; coverage is endpoint-specific | 10 | 3 | 7 | 210 | Centralise resource authorization, derive tenant/account/customer from the loaded resource, deny by default, add a matrix of cross-tenant/customer/support/admin tests, and audit authorization failures. |
| F-15 | Closure proceeds with money remaining or closure requests become unsafe/stuck | Account balance is a stale copy and must be checked against the ledger; this repository exposes request/list only and has no visible approver decision/close operation | Funds may be stranded, or customers cannot close accounts; approvers, operations and complaints teams lack a complete workflow | Pending-request conflict and tenant-scoped approver list; no ledger-zero check or completion path is implemented here | 10 | 4 | 9 | 360 | Implement the complete two-person workflow, validate current ledger balance is exactly zero immediately before close, prevent payments/closure races, record decision audit data, and alert on aged pending requests. |
| F-16 | Statements or regulatory return are missing/incorrect | `Statement` persistence exists but no generation, ledger close ingestion, statement endpoint or monthly job is present in the inspected code | Customers lack records; the regulatory return due by the fifth working day is wrong or late; complaints evidence is incomplete | Fixture data and repository only; no freshness/completeness control | 9 | 5 | 10 | 450 | Define statement ownership and implement an idempotent monthly close from ledger closing balances, completeness checks, immutable/auditable versions, customer access and return reconciliation. Treat missing statements as a release gap if in scope for this service. |
| F-17 | Authentication or authorization configuration fails open or becomes unavailable | Wrong identity public key/rotation configuration, malformed claims, or a permissive endpoint rule; all calls depend on the identity service and signing key | Customers cannot access accounts, or—if misconfigured—unauthorised users can access or mutate them | JWT signature/expiry validation, required bearer token, role/scope checks, health endpoint; no key-rotation or fail-closed integration evidence in repo | 10 | 3 | 6 | 180 | Test key rotation, malformed/missing tenant/roles/scopes and identity outage; fail closed, monitor 401/403 spikes separately, and make key refresh/configuration observable and deploy-safe. |
| F-18 | Database/service outage or data loss makes the API unavailable or loses records | The sample configuration uses in-memory H2 with `create-drop`; production durability, HA, backups, migrations and capacity controls are not described | Mobile/back-office outage; missing payment, case, note or complaint records; operations cannot reconcile or meet SLA | Spring error handling and health endpoint only; no backup/restore, readiness, connection-pool or SLO evidence in repo | 10 | 3 | 8 | 240 | Confirm production database architecture, durable backups and restore drills, migrations, HA/failover, readiness checks, pool/timeouts, and RPO/RTO alarms. Never use `create-drop` or in-memory persistence in production. |
| F-19 | Time-dependent behaviour is wrong around timezone/DST or uses wall-clock inconsistently | Payment flow uses injected UK `Clock`, but closure/case timestamps use `Instant.now()`; job/cut-off/holiday logic can disagree at boundaries | Wrong cut-off, execution date, audit timestamps or statement period; payment delay or incorrect evidence | UK timezone on scheduled job and injected clock in payment paths; no DST/clock consistency check for all domains | 7 | 4 | 7 | 196 | Inject the clock everywhere, store UTC instants plus explicit business timezone where needed, centralise business-date calculation, and test DST, midnight, cut-off and leap/holiday boundaries. |
| F-20 | Operational exceptions are not detected or acted on within the required window | Logs are the main signal; no metrics/alerts/dashboards are evident for unknown scheme outcomes, held payments, `POSTING_FAILED`, overdue queue, notification failure or reconciliation breaks | Problems persist until customers or finance discover them; payment, fraud four-hour and 07:00 reconciliation commitments are missed | `POSTING_FAILED` and overdue `QUEUED` are named reports; individual warnings are logged | 9 | 6 | 9 | 486 | Define SLIs/SLOs and alerts for each state transition and external dependency, publish correlation/payment references, create daily reconciliation dashboards, and assign on-call ownership/runbooks. |

## Release priorities

1. Block release on F-01, F-03, F-05, F-06 and F-07: these can enable unscreened, duplicated, falsely reported or financially unsafe payments.
2. Before enabling production traffic, close F-09–F-12 and F-20 with durable state transitions, idempotent recovery and actionable alerts.
3. Confirm whether statements and closure completion are in this service's release scope. If they are, F-15 and F-16 are release blockers because the repository does not contain the required end-to-end controls.

The RPNs should be recalculated after the actions are implemented and after production occurrence/detection data is available.
