# Accounts service FMEA

Reviewed 2026-11-04. This assessment covers the customer-facing API, persistence, scheduled payment work, and the
integrations described in `okf/`. The service is the system of record for the customer's view, but the core ledger is
the system of record for money. Scores use a 1--10 scale: severity (S) is impact if the failure occurs, occurrence (O)
is expected frequency without the recommended action, and detection (D) is difficulty of detecting the failure before
customer or downstream impact (10 is hardest). `RPN = S x O x D`; higher RPNs are the first remediation candidates.

The scores are relative operational triage scores, not a substitute for measured incident data. A score is based on the
current implementation and known gaps in the repository knowledge bundle.

| FMEA-01 | Payment is sent without fraud screening | `PaymentService` calls the dispatcher directly; the `FraudScreeningClient` exists but is not invoked. | A prohibited or suspicious payment can be sent. Customers may lose funds; the fraud team and compliance process lose the mandatory screening trail. | Code/configuration test that asserts every payment is screened; production metric for screening decision per payment and alert on any missing decision. | 10 | 7 | 8 | **560** | Put screening before scheme submission. Fail closed on timeout/error; map `ACCEPT` to continue, `REFER` to `HELD_FOR_REVIEW`, and `REJECT` to `REJECTED` with reason `FRAUD`. Add integration and negative-path tests. |
| FMEA-02 | Scheme accepts a payment but the service reports failure or loses it | A scheme timeout may mean accepted; `SchemeTimeoutException` is not caught and no instruction-status query is used. The surrounding transaction can roll back the local payment after the scheme has accepted it. | Customer may see an error and retry, causing confusion or a duplicate; the service has no record to reconcile. Finance sees settlement without a matching payment and operations must reconstruct it. | Reconcile scheme settlement against payment references; alert on gateway timeout rate and unmatched scheme instructions; customer retry/complaint signals. | 10 | 5 | 9 | **450** | Persist the payment and stable scheme reference before submission; catch timeout, query `GET /v2/instructions/{reference}`, and place unresolved cases in an explicit review state. Never automatically create a new scheme reference. |
| FMEA-03 | Same payment is posted to the ledger twice | A retry generates a new random `ledgerReference` in `PaymentDispatcher`; it does not preserve the original idempotency key. A ledger timeout may have booked the first attempt. | Customer balance is debited twice in the core ledger. Finance's 07:00 reconciliation breaks; operations and complaints teams must investigate and remediate. | Daily ledger-to-scheme reconciliation by payment/reference; alert on more than one ledger posting for a payment or account; compare ledger postings with `ledgerReference`. | 10 | 4 | 8 | **320** | Generate and persist `ledgerReference` before the first post and reuse it for every retry. Add a unique database constraint and idempotency tests for timeout-before/after-booking. |
| FMEA-04 | Payment sent but ledger posting fails or remains unresolved | Ledger is unavailable or times out after booking; the dispatcher marks `POSTING_FAILED`, but there is no automatic status query/retry and operations must act from an exceptions report. | Scheme payment can settle while the service/ledger view is inconsistent; customer balance and transaction history are wrong. Finance gets a reconciliation break and operations has a manual repair queue. | Alert on `POSTING_FAILED`; daily comparison of scheme settlement and ledger postings; age alert for unresolved exceptions. | 10 | 5 | 6 | **300** | Implement a durable retry/reconciliation worker using the same reference, with bounded retries and a dead-letter/manual-review state. Make the exceptions report complete, actionable, and monitored for age. |
| FMEA-05 | A payment is accepted against an incorrect balance or daily limit | `accounts.balance` is a copy refreshed from the ledger at 02:00; concurrent requests can both pass checks and debit a stale in-memory/database value. | Customer may overspend or receive an incorrect available balance; ledger, finance reconciliation, support, and complaints records diverge. | Compare payment totals and ledger balances; alert on negative/over-limit accounts; concurrency/load tests and optimistic-lock conflict metrics. | 9 | 6 | 7 | **378** | Treat the ledger as authoritative for funds checks, or use an atomic reservation/row lock with versioning and a clear reconciliation process. | FMEA-06 | Queued payment is sent on the wrong day or not sent | `WorkingDays` excludes weekends but not bank holidays; the 06:00 scheduled job may be disabled, fail, or run without a durable run/lock record. | Payment is delayed or rejected with scheme `DT01`; customers miss promised timing. | FMEA-07 | A payment is rejected or queued because local time/cut-off is wrong | Clock/zone configuration differs from UK time, code uses an un-injected `now`, or cut-off/calendar configuration is stale. | Same-day payment may be delayed or sent after the scheme cut-off and rejected. ## Immediate release actions

Before release, the highest-value controls are to wire in fail-closed fraud screening (FMEA-01), make scheme and ledger
operations recoverable with stable idempotency references (FMEA-02--04), and gate closure on a live ledger balance
(FMEA-11). In parallel, add overdue-queue/job monitoring and bank-holiday handling (FMEA-06), because these are likely to
create customer-visible failures without an obvious API error.

The operational minimum for launch is a dashboard and alert set covering: payment state counts and age; fraud decisions;
scheme timeouts/status-query outcomes; `POSTING_FAILED`; queue age and job runs; ledger-to-scheme reconciliation;
statement completeness; closure exceptions; 401/403/5xx and dependency latency; and notification failures. Each alert
needs an owner and a runbook, with finance operations owning reconciliation exceptions and the fraud team owning
`HELD_FOR_REVIEW` age.
