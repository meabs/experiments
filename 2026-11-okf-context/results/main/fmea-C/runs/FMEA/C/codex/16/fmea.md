# Accounts service FMEA

Review date: 4 November 2026  
Scope: the customer and staff API, persistence, authentication/authorisation, payment dispatch, scheduled queue processing, and the four external integrations. This is a design-and-code review based on `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, the implementation and API tests; occurrence scores are qualitative until production incident and volume data are available.

## Scoring

Severity (S) measures the worst credible customer, financial, regulatory or operational consequence. Occurrence (O) estimates likelihood under normal operation and known failure exposure. Detection (D) measures how difficult it is to detect before material impact (1 = immediately visible/prevented, 10 = likely to remain hidden). RPN = S × O × D. Scores are relative prioritisation aids, not probabilities.

## Highest-priority release actions

The highest RPN items are F-01 (fraud control absent), F-03/F-14 (uncertain external outcomes and transaction rollback), F-02 (fraud decision semantics), F-05 (duplicate queue dispatch), F-13 (client retry duplication), F-04 (ledger timeout recovery), and F-06 (stale/concurrent balance). These should have owners, acceptance tests and operational dashboards before release. F-17 and F-18 are control gaps that should be tracked as separate delivery work if those workflows are in the release scope.

At minimum, the release gate should require: fraud screening before any scheme call; durable idempotent payment state and references; scheme and ledger uncertainty recovery; atomic fund/limit reservation; queued-job claiming and overdue alerts; bank-holiday-aware calendar logic; three-way reconciliation; and tests proving tenant isolation, notification redaction and timeout-before/after-acceptance behaviour.
