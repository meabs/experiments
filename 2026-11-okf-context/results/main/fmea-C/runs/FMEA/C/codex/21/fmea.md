# Accounts service FMEA

Date: 4 November 2026  
Scope: the Spring Boot accounts-service API, its database, scheduled payment dispatch, and the scheme, fraud, ledger and notification integrations.

## Method

Scores are on a 1–10 scale. Severity (S) measures the impact if the failure occurs; occurrence (O) measures the expected likelihood under normal operation and known gaps; detection (D) measures how difficult it is to detect before customer or downstream impact (10 is least detectable). RPN = S × O × D. The scores are relative prioritisation aids, not estimates of loss or frequency. The highest RPNs and all fraud, duplicate-payment, data-protection and cross-tenant risks should be treated as release blockers until controlled.

The assessment reflects the implementation and documented system behaviour, including known gaps. Existing tests use signed tokens, a mutable UK clock and fakes for the four external systems, but they do not constitute production monitoring or reconciliation.

## Release priorities

1. Block release until F-01 is implemented and tested end-to-end, including unavailable, `REFER` and `REJECT` outcomes.
2. Fix F-02 immediately as a data-protection issue.
3. Make scheme and ledger outcomes durable and recoverable (F-03, F-04, F-05 and F-10); add payment/scheme/ledger reconciliation before enabling retries.
4. Add atomic balance/limit reservation and authoritative-ledger controls (F-06 and F-07).
5. Replace weekday-only scheduling with the bank-holiday calendar and make the queued job observable and restart-safe (F-08 and F-09).
6. Complete closure controls against the ledger (F-15), then implement the privacy, tenant-isolation, clock and production persistence controls.

## Minimum operational dashboard and alerts

Track payment counts and age by status (`RECEIVED`, `QUEUED`, `SUBMITTED`, `POSTING_FAILED`, `HELD_FOR_REVIEW`, `REJECTED`, `COMPLETED`), screening decision coverage, scheme and ledger timeout/error rates, unknown external references, ledger-versus-scheme reconciliation breaks, account-copy freshness/variance, overdue queued payments, job heartbeat/duration, notification failures, 401/403 anomalies, and closure requests pending/non-zero. Alerts should link to payment id, scheme reference and ledger reference without logging full account numbers or sort codes.
