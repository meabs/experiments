A shopper reports: "I put 2 of one product and 3 of another in my cart. Each line shows the price of a single unit instead of the line amount, and the cart subtotal and total are much too low, as if I had bought just one of each."

Each cart line's subtotal must be the unit price times the quantity, and the cart's subtotal and total must count every unit. This applies to the storefront cart API (GET /api/v1/cart/{code} and the responses when items are added).
