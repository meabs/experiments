Implemented goodwill credits.

- Added `POST /api/accounts/{accountId}/goodwill-credits`.
- Assigned support and tenant admins may create credits; customers cannot.
- Validates positive amounts with at most two decimals and trimmed reasons of 1–500 characters.
- Credits up to £100 are immediately `PAID`; larger credits are `PENDING`.
- Added `POST /api/goodwill-credits/{creditId}/approve` for same-tenant approvers.
- Approval enforces creator separation, cross-tenant 404s, and non-pending 409s.
- Paid credits update balance and create a `Goodwill credit` transaction atomically.
- Closed accounts return 409.

`mvn -o -q test` passes.
