Implemented goodwill credits.

Highlights:

- Added `POST /api/accounts/{accountId}/goodwill-credits`.
- Requires `accounts:write`.
- Authorized for assigned support staff and tenant admins only.
- Validates positive amounts with up to 2 decimal places and trimmed reasons of 1–500 characters.
- Rejects closed or inaccessible accounts appropriately.
- Persists goodwill-credit records with ID, creator, timestamp, amount, and reason.
- Atomically updates balance and books a positive `Goodwill credit` transaction.
- Added pessimistic account locking for concurrent credits.

All existing tests pass with:

```bash
mvn -o -q test
```
