Implemented goodwill credits with the agreed workflow:

- `POST /api/accounts/{accountId}/goodwill-credits`
  - Staff/admin authorization and `accounts:write`
  - Input validation and reason trimming
  - ≤£100 credits are immediately paid
  - >£100 credits are created as `PENDING`
  - Returns `201` with credit details and status
- `POST /api/goodwill-credits/{creditId}/approve`
  - Requires `APPROVER` and `accounts:write`
  - Enforces tenant boundaries and separation of duties
  - Pays pending credits atomically
- Paid credits update account balance and create a `Goodwill credit` transaction.
- Closed accounts and repeat approvals return conflicts.
