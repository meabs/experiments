1. **Which staff roles may freeze/unfreeze?**  
   Recommendation: require `accounts:write` plus either `SUPPORT` or `ADMIN`; support users may act only on accounts assigned to them, while tenant admins may act on any account in their tenant. An `APPROVER`-only user would not qualify (users who also have SUPPORT/ADMIN would qualify).

2. **What should happen when the caller cannot act on the account?**  
   Recommendation: preserve the existing resource-hiding rule—return 404 when the account is outside the caller’s visibility/tenant; return 403 when a visible account is owned by a customer or otherwise not actionable by that role.

3. **Should the reason be persisted, and should it be returned?**  
   Recommendation: persist the trimmed reason as an internal account field for back-office traceability, but do not add it to `AccountView`; responses must remain exactly the GET account shape, so customers never see it. (This requires a schema/entity field and a fixture-compatible default.)

4. **How should repeated or incompatible transitions behave?**  
   Recommendation: return 409 when freezing an already `FROZEN` account, unfreezing an `OPEN` account, or attempting either operation on a `CLOSED` account. Only `OPEN → FROZEN` and `FROZEN → OPEN` are valid.

5. **What HTTP success status should the POST endpoints use?**  
   Recommendation: `200 OK`, because each operation returns the updated account representation and is not creating a new resource.

6. **Should the trimmed reason be what is stored?**  
   Recommendation: yes—trim leading/trailing whitespace, validate the trimmed value is 1–500 characters, and store that trimmed value. Character counting would follow Java/Spring validation’s usual character semantics.

7. **Should freezing change any behavior beyond the status?**  
   Recommendation: no additional effects (for example, do not alter visibility or transaction reads); preserve all existing behavior except exposing the new status and enforcing the transition rules above.
