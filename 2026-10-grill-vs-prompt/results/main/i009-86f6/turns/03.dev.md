Yes: over £100 it's saved as pending (status PENDING), still 201, and nothing is paid until it's approved. An approver approves it with POST /api/goodwill-credits/{creditId}/approve (no body): it needs the APPROVER role in the credit's bank (other roles 403, another bank 404). Approving pays it (balance up, the Goodwill credit transaction booked), sets the status to PAID and returns the credit; approving one that isn't pending is 409. Including status in the POST response is fine.
