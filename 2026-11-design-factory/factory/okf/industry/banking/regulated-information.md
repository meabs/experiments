# Regulated information by product

A design aid, written November 2026. **Confidence**: *checked* = confirmed against a regulator's page (by search
summary) on 11 November 2026; *known* = from the author's knowledge of the rule, not re-checked; *convention* = good
practice rather than a rule. Everything goes in the legal register for sign-off; wording in prototypes is draft.

Overall duties that shape every screen (FCA Consumer Duty, PRIN 2A; *checked* for the consumer understanding
outcome): communications must be likely to be understood by the customers they are for; equip people to make
effective, timely and properly informed decisions; avoid foreseeable harm; no unreasonable barriers to leaving,
complaining or getting support. Promotions and communications must be fair, clear and not misleading (*known*).

## Payments

| Information | Where | Rule or basis | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Name check result (match, close match, no match, not available) before a first payment to a new payee | after entering payee details | Confirmation of Payee (PSR direction) | checked |
| A warning of the risk when the customer continues after a no-match or close-match result | on the result state | Confirmation of Payee / scam prevention | known |
| Amount and payee repeated at approval | approval step | strong customer authentication, dynamic linking (Payment Services Regulations 2017) | known |
| Any charge and when the payment will arrive | review, before confirming | Payment Services Regulations 2017, information before execution | known |
| A reference and confirmation of amount and date afterwards | result | Payment Services Regulations 2017, information after execution | known |
| How to report a scam; reimbursement exists up to a limit (£85,000 at launch in October 2024) | help and warning content; never as a promise | PSR APP scam reimbursement rules | checked (limit at launch); check current rule |

## Savings

| Information | Where | Rule or basis | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Summary box in the standard form, with the rate inside it | before the customer commits | BCOBS 2.2A, BCOBS 2 Annex 1 | checked |

| Terms available to read and keep before agreeing | before commitment | BCOBS 4 | known |

## Borrowing (overdrafts and other credit)

| Information | Where | Rule or basis | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| An overdraft is borrowing | wherever overdrafts are offered | BCOBS 2.2B | checked |
| Eligibility tool that does not affect the credit file | before applying | BCOBS 8.3 (larger current-account providers) | checked (tool exists); soft-search wording is convention |
| Representative APR; cost shown so it can be compared | wherever the price is shown | FCA overdraft pricing rules 2019 to 2020; CONC 3 for credit promotions | known |
| A cost calculator or cost example in pounds | product and offer screens | BCOBS 8 | known |
| Affordability is assessed; explain why income and outgoings are asked | application | CONC 5.2A (creditworthiness) | known |
| Pre-contract information and the agreement, to read and keep, before agreeing | offer | Consumer Credit Act 1974 and CONC 4 | known |
| Right to withdraw within 14 days | offer or result | Consumer Credit Act 1974, section 66A | known; check current rule |
| If declined on credit-reference data: say so and name the agency | decline | Consumer Credit Act 1974, section 157; CONC | known |
| Help for people in financial difficulty | throughout | Consumer Duty; CONC 7 | known |
