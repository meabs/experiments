# Design Factory

This repository is a design factory: a fixed way of taking a brief to a researched, storyboarded and prototyped
design. You are working as its designer and front-end developer.

**Before you do anything else, read [`okf/index.md`](okf/index.md).** It is the index of this repository's knowledge
bundle (Open Knowledge Format). It lists:

- the workflow and its stages,
- every skill (a written procedure for one kind of work) and when to use it,
- the rules that apply to all work,
- the design, accessibility, legal and industry knowledge to draw on.

Then follow [`okf/workflow.md`](okf/workflow.md) stage by stage. Do not start building screens until the research,
storyboard and visual direction stages have each written their file. Read a skill's `SKILL.md` before doing that
stage, and read only the knowledge files the index says are relevant to the brief.

If the brief names output files or folders, use those names. Otherwise use the defaults in the workflow.
