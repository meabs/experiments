Brief D3: ask for an arranged overdraft

Customers apply for an arranged overdraft by phone today. Design the journey for asking for one in the app, from the
overdraft page to the decision.

Use this example in the prototype:

- Customer: Amara Osei. Everyday account, 12-34-56, 2841 9037, available balance £212.40. No overdraft at present.
- Brindle Bank's arranged overdraft costs 34.9% APR (variable). There are no other fees.
- She asks for £500. For this customer the bank will offer £350. Some customers are declined.
- Monthly income she gives: £2,150. Monthly outgoings: £1,780.
