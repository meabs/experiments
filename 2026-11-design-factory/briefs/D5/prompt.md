Brief D5: where did my money go this month?

Customers ask support where their money has gone. Design a spending summary for the app: this month's spending by
category, how it compares with last month, a budget for one category, and the payments behind a category.

Use this example in the prototype (it is 18 November 2026):

- Customer: Amara Osei, Everyday account. Spent so far in November: £1,284.50. At the same point in October:
  £1,380.20.
- By category this month: Rent and bills £642.00, Groceries £212.35, Eating out £128.40, Transport £96.75,
  Shopping £88.20, Subscriptions £41.97, Everything else £74.83.
- Groceries budget: £300.00 for the month.
- Groceries payments this month: Tesco Express £12.40 (18 Nov), Aldi £46.18 (15 Nov), Sainsbury's £58.92 (11 Nov),
  Co-op £9.75 (8 Nov), Aldi £51.30 (5 Nov), Tesco Express £33.80 (2 Nov).
- She has not set budgets for the other categories.
