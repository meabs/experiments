Brief D4: my card is missing

Customers who lose their debit card have to ring a phone line. Design the journey in the app for a customer whose
card is missing, from the card screen to the card being safe and a replacement on its way.

Use this example in the prototype:

- Customer: Amara Osei. Brindle debit card ending 4321, linked to her Everyday account.
- Home address on file: 14 Larch Close, Leeds, LS6 2AB.
- Recent card payments: Tesco Express £12.40 (today), TfL Travel £8.10 (yesterday), NVX*DIGITAL £74.99 (yesterday),
  Corner Coffee £3.80 (2 days ago). She does not recognise the NVX*DIGITAL payment.
- A replacement card takes 3 to 5 working days.
