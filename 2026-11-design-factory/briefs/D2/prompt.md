Brief D2: open a savings account

Customers with a current account can only open a savings account by phone today. Design the journey for opening an
Easy Saver in the app, from the product's page to the account being open with money in it.

Use this example in the prototype:

- Customer: Amara Osei. Everyday account, 12-34-56, 2841 9037, available balance £1,842.16.
- Easy Saver: 4.10% AER (4.02% gross), variable. Interest is paid monthly. Open with £1 or more, up to £250,000.
  Withdraw at any time without charge.
- She opens it with £500.00 from her Everyday account.
