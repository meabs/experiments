Brief D1: pay someone new

Customers can already pay people on their payee list. Paying someone for the first time is where most customers give
up or ring support. Design the journey for a one-off payment to a person or business the customer has never paid
before, from tapping "Pay someone new" to seeing what happened to the payment.

Use this example in the prototype:

- Customer: Amara Osei. Paying from her Everyday account, 12-34-56, 2841 9037, available balance £1,842.16.
- She is paying her builder a deposit: she types the name "Dan Kowalczyk", sort code 65-43-21, account number
  5519 2046, amount £650.00, reference "Kitchen deposit".
- The receiving account is actually held in the name "D Kowalczyk Building Ltd".
- Her daily limit for payments to new payees is £1,000.
