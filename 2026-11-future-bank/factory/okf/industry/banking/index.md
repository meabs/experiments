# Banking (UK retail)

For briefs about current accounts, payments, savings, borrowing and cards for personal customers in the UK. Use with
the [banking-design skill](../../../skills/banking-design/SKILL.md).

Always read:

- [Money and trust](/industry/banking/money-and-trust.md) - how money, accounts and status are shown; tone; the
  banking components; glossary of plain words.
- [Regulated information](/industry/banking/regulated-information.md) - what a screen must carry for each product,
  with the rule it comes from and how sure this file is.
- [Customers in vulnerable circumstances](/industry/banking/vulnerable-customers.md) - who struggles and what the
  design does about it.

Then the one that matches the journey:

- [Payments](/industry/banking/payments.md) - paying someone, new payees, name checks, approval, pending and failed
  payments, scams.
- [Savings](/industry/banking/savings.md) - choosing and opening a savings account, rates, the summary box,
  protection, withdrawals.
- [Borrowing](/industry/banking/borrowing.md) - overdrafts, credit cards and loans: eligibility, cost, applying,
  being declined, money worries.
- [Cards and security](/industry/banking/cards-and-security.md) - freezing, lost or stolen cards, replacement,
  disputed transactions, signing in and approving.
- [Accounts and insights](/industry/banking/accounts-and-insights.md) - home screens, balances, transaction lists,
  spending summaries and budgets.

All regulatory points here are a design aid, written in November 2026, status draft. Each carries a confidence
note. Nothing here is legal advice; use the legal-review skill's register for anything a screen depends on.
