Ticket: let staff close support cases.

When a support case is resolved, the support user handling it (its assignee) or an admin closes it with a short
resolution. Support users who aren't the case's assignee, customers, and staff of other banks can't close it.

Add `POST /api/support/cases/{caseId}/close` with the body `{"resolution": "..."}` (1 to 2000 characters after
trimming, otherwise 400). The case's status becomes `CLOSED`. It returns `{"id", "accountId", "subject", "status",
"openedAt"}`.

Follow the existing conventions (README.md) and keep the existing behaviour and tests working.
