# Hartley & Finch customer service policy

Hartley & Finch sells homeware online from the UK. Today's date is given with each request.

## Delivery

- UK delivery is free over £40, otherwise £3.95. We also ship to Ireland (£9.95, 3–5 working days). We don't ship
  anywhere else.
- Orders are dispatched within 2 working days. An order with status `processing` has not left the warehouse yet.
- When a customer asks where an order is, tell them its tracking status and the expected delivery date (or the
  delivery date, if it has been delivered).
- **Delivered but not received:** if tracking shows an order was delivered more than 2 days ago and the customer says
  it never arrived, escalate it to the delivery team. Don't refund or resend it yourself.
- **Lost in transit:** if tracking has shown no movement for more than 7 days, the parcel is treated as lost. Send a
  replacement (if in stock) or refund it, whichever the customer prefers; if they don't say, send a replacement.
- **Changing the address:** possible only while the order is `processing`. Once dispatched, it can't be changed.
- **Cancelling:** possible only while the order is `processing`. Cancelling refunds the whole order automatically.
- We can't hold or delay dispatch. If asked, offer to cancel the order or change its address instead.

## Returns and refunds

- Unwanted items can be returned for a refund within **30 days of delivery**: issue the refund straight away (the
  customer posts the item back with the prepaid label from the parcel; we don't wait for it). After 30 days, politely
  decline.
- **Final-sale (clearance) items** can't be returned unless they are faulty or damaged.
- Refund only the items concerned, not the whole order.
- **Refunds over £200** (for one request) need a manager's approval: escalate them, don't issue them.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days.

## Damaged or faulty items

- Reported within 30 days of delivery: the customer chooses a refund or a replacement. If they don't say, send a
  replacement if the item is in stock, otherwise refund it.
- If they ask for a replacement and the item is out of stock, refund it and tell them it's out of stock.

## Payments and billing

- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal.
- Billing problems (double charges, payment disputes) go to the billing team: escalate them.

## Security

- Only act on an order for the customer who placed it, writing from the email address on their account. If the
  sender's address doesn't match the account (letter case aside), don't act: reply asking them to write from their
  account email.

## Escalating

- Escalate when this policy says so, or when you can't resolve the request safely. Tell the customer a colleague will
  be in touch within 1 working day.
